ABOUT SASEP

South Asia Science Education Programme (SASEP) was conceptualised in 2020 at CERN with an aim to build a global collaboration platform to discuss these futuristic advancements with leading Global Scientists , CXOs from top MNCs and thought leaders in the educational landscape who are working towards understanding our past and visualising our future. SASEP achieves this by organising thought provoking sessions and discussions to navigate these futuristic developments in a manner that is both fun and unconventional. If you are an Educational Thought Leader who is keen on exploring the latest science, technology and innovation trends, SASEP is a conference for you and the experts in your school.
